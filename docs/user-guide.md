# Bike Trip Journal: User Guide

This guide is for friends and family following a trip, and for the people riding it. You don't need to be technical.

## The short version

- **Following a trip:** open the site and pick a public trip. No account needed.
- **Adding stops and photos:** create an account, ask to join the trip, and wait for a leader to approve you.
- **Starting a trip:** create an account, then tap **Create a trip**. You become its leader.

## Creating an account

1. Tap **Create an account** on the front page (or **Sign in**, then **Create an account**).
2. Enter a **Display name**, a **Username** and a **Password**.
   - Your display name is shown on the photos you add and to trip leaders. You can't change it later.
   - Your username is private. It's only used to sign in.
   - Your password must be 15 to 128 characters. A short sentence you'll remember works well.
3. Tap **Create account**. You're signed in straight away.

### Keep your recovery code safe

Next you'll see a **recovery code**. This matters.

- It's shown **once**. Nobody can show it to you again.
- It's the only way back in if you forget your password. There's no email reset.
- Copy it, or download it as a text file, and keep it in your password manager or written down somewhere safe.
- Tick **I've saved my recovery code** to continue.

**If you forget your password:** on the sign-in page tap **Forgot your password? Use your recovery code**. Enter your username, your recovery code and a new password. Using the code gives you a **new** recovery code, which is shown once, and the old one stops working. You're signed out on your other devices.

**If you've lost the code too:** ask the person who runs the site to reset your account.

You can also get a new recovery code any time while signed in: open **Account** and tap **Get a new recovery code**. The old code stops working.

Signing in and resetting a password both need a connection.

## Public and private trips

Every trip is either **public** or **private**.

- **Public trips** appear on the front page. Anyone can open them without an account.
- **Private trips** are invisible to anyone who isn't a member. To everyone else it looks like the trip doesn't exist ("Trip not found"). Nobody can ask to join a private trip.

### The delay

Public trips have a **delay**. The people on the bike choose it. A new trip starts at 24 hours. People who aren't members only see a stop after the delay has passed. That way nobody can see where the riders are right now, or where they're camping tonight.

- Members (riders and leaders) see stops straight away. In the stop list, a recent stop shows "Public from ..." with the time it becomes visible to everyone else.
- A leader can change the delay in the trip's settings, from 0 to 168 hours. With 0, stops are public as soon as they're sent.
- What the public sees: the map, stops, photos and bikes, plus riders' display names. They never see usernames, the member list or join requests.

## Creating a trip

1. Sign in, then tap **Create a trip** on the front page.
2. Enter a **Trip name** and a **Start date**.
3. Choose **Public** (the default) or **Private**.
4. Tap **Create trip**.

You're now the trip's **leader**. If you pick Public, check your first stop before you add it. Trips often start at someone's home, and people outside the trip will see each stop's exact location once the delay passes.

If other people are riding with you, keep the trip public until they've joined. Nobody can ask to join a private trip. You can make it private later in the trip settings, and members keep their access.

Share the trip's link with your riders. They sign in and tap **Ask to join this trip**.

## Asking to join a trip

1. Sign in and open the trip.
2. Tap **Ask to join this trip** and add a short message if you like, such as which bike you ride. Leaders see your display name and message.
3. Tap **Send request**.

You'll see **Request sent**. Nothing else is needed. **Add stop** appears on the trip once a leader approves you. Leaders may be out riding, so approval isn't instant. You can **Cancel request** whenever you like. Your requests, and what happened to them, are listed under **Account**.

If a leader doesn't approve a request, you can ask again, but not straight away. If it's too soon, the app tells you when you send.

## If you're a leader

Leaders decide who can add stops and photos. Open the **Members** tab on the trip.

- **Requests:** see each person's display name and message. Tap **Approve** or **Reject**. If you have several, tick them and approve or reject them together. **Reject and block** (under the **...** menu) stops that person asking again until you unblock them from the **Blocked** list.
- **Make leader** promotes a rider. Every leader has the same powers, and **leaders can't remove each other**. A leader can only stop being a leader by stepping down.
- **Revoke** removes a rider. They can ask to join again after 7 days.
- **Leave trip** and **Step down** are for yourself. **The last leader can't leave or step down.** Promote another rider first.
- **Trip settings** let you rename the trip, switch between public and private, and change the delay. Making a private trip public shares everything already added, not only new stops.

Riders see the same member list, but can only leave the trip themselves.

## Old links

Earlier, trips were shared with a "rider link" and a "viewer link". Those links still open, but they're **read-only** now. You can look at the trip, but not change it. You'll see the notice **This is an old trip link**.

- **Not signed in:** you can keep viewing. To add stops, sign in or create an account.
- **Signed in, not a member:** tap **Ask to join as a rider**. This sends a request to the leaders. An old link never gives you access by itself. A leader still has to approve you.
- **Already a member:** tap **Open trip** to use the new view.

If you get "This link can't be used to join", ask a leader of the trip for the rider link, or to make the trip public.

If you have an old link, you can paste it in under **Have an old trip link?** on the front page.

## Install it on your home screen (riders: do this)

**iPhone:** open the site in Safari, tap **Share**, then tap **Add to Home Screen**.
**Android:** open the site in Chrome, open the menu, then tap **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.

On iPhone, the installed app keeps its **own** sign-in and its **own** unsent stops, separate from Safari. So:

1. Open the installed app and **sign in there**, even if you're already signed in in Safari.
2. **Always add stops from the installed app, not from Safari.** Stops waiting to send are stored in the app you used to add them. A stop added in Safari can't be seen, or sent, by the installed app.

## Adding a stop

You need to be a rider or leader on the trip.

1. On the trip, tap **Add stop**.
2. The app gets your position from GPS. If it can't, tap the map to set your location. That stop is marked "approximate location".
3. Enter a **Name** (required) and any **Notes**.
4. Pick or take one or more photos. They're shrunk on your phone first. Tap **Remove** if you picked the wrong one.
5. Tap **Save stop**.

Saving never needs a signal. The stop and photos are saved on your phone first and sent when there's a connection. Don't delete the app or clear its data while items are waiting, or they'll be lost.

## The notice about unsent items

A notice at the top of each screen shows anything that hasn't been sent yet.

- **"Waiting to send: 2 stops, 5 photos".** These send by themselves when you have a connection. Nothing to do.
- **"Sign in to send 3 items".** Your session ended while items were waiting. Sign in and they send. They're not lost.
- **"2 items were saved by another account on this phone. Sign in as that account to send them."** Someone else signed in on this phone and added these. They're held, not deleted. They send when that account signs in. They never send under your name.
- **"<name> still trying: ..."**. This has failed 10 or more times. Usually a weak signal. It keeps trying.
- **"<name> could not be sent: ..."** with **Dismiss**. The site refused it, so retrying won't help. It stays on your phone until you tap **Dismiss**. If a stop fails, its photos fail with it.

If a **photo** couldn't be sent, tap **Save photo to this device** before you dismiss it. The photo goes to your phone's downloads or gallery, so you don't lose it.

Signing out keeps your waiting items on the phone.

## Bikes

Tap **Bikes** on a trip to see each rider's bike: make, model, year and specs. Riders and leaders can add or edit bikes.

**Bikes need a connection.** Unlike stops, they aren't saved for later. If you're offline you'll see "You're offline — try again when connected." What you typed stays in the form, so tap save again once you have a signal.

## If something looks wrong

- **"Waking up the server..."** The site sleeps when nobody's using it. Wait a moment.
- **"Trip not found".** The link is wrong, or the trip is private and you're not a member.
- Viewing a trip needs a connection. Adding a stop doesn't.
